# E09 — Monochrome AI explainer, 3D icons (720×1280, 60fps, 19.3s)
Format: **Faceless monochrome motion graphic, one hero image per idea.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0s: frame already full: giant ghosted "AI" (light grey, ~35% height) behind a B&W cyborg-brain head, two **3D glossy black app-icon tiles** (ChatGPT logo) at top-left & bottom-right corners, slowly floating/rotating.
- 0.3s: solid dark "AI" appears over the ghost, then tiny "IS", then "EVERYWHERE" spaced caps (word-by-word).
- Whole scene **slow push-in + slight float** for 2.2s; nothing cuts.
- 2.3s: hard cut to "YOUR / PHONE" — B&W hand holding phone, UI notification cards stack in one by one, grey circles decor, Siri-like sphere bottom-right.
- Changes in 3s: 4. Calm but always moving.

## Structure
- One scene per claim, 1.5–2.5s each: AI phone → AI robot "AI Powered" (cute 3D robot) → "ART" (designer at desk in big grey circle) → "AI create this in SECONDS" (colourful product image = **only colour in the video**, used as proof) → "DRIVING" (Tesla, text then car slides) → "AI sees roads / BETTER THAN HUMANS" (Tesla FSD screen footage in rounded card) → "HEALTHCARE?" (3D clipboard + crosses) → news-article screenshot card ("This AI saved hundreds of lives from sepsis…") with AI-doctor figure → "ARE YOU READY… FOR AN AI FUTURE?" → "FOLLOW FOR MORE".
- **Proof cards**: real article screenshot / real footage inside rounded cards = credibility beats.

## Visual rules
- Strict greyscale palette; colour reserved for the "wow" proof.
- Type: heavy italic/extended grotesk for keyword + light serif italic ("Powered", "SECONDS") as contrast pair; small caps tags.
- Elements: 3D clay/glossy icons, soft grey circles, giant ghost words behind objects.
- Persistent small brand pill bottom-left.

## Engine needs
Ghost-word background, 3D icon tiles (pre-rendered PNGs + float), stacked UI notification cards, article screenshot card component, greyscale filter on all assets except proof.

"AI in healthcare" educational series — real news screenshots as proof cards, greyscale + teal accent.
