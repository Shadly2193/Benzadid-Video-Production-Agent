# E12 — Real-estate presenter with floating pinned cards (720×1280, 30fps, 36.6s)
Format: **Standing presenter (green-screen/composited set) + floating 3D cards + property renders.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0s: presenter full-body, walking slightly forward; background = gym with palm-beach wall (likely keyed).
- 0.2s: "What if" **heavy italic yellow (#FFD400) with glow + dark drop shadow**, on chest, word pop with overshoot.
- 0.5–1.3s: **photo cards with red push-pins** fly in from the right, tilted ~10°, white borders, stacking in an arc **behind the presenter's head** (person matte) and the set rotates slightly (2.5D camera).
- 0.8–2.0s: small white caps line "YOU COULD ESCAPE TO" + "Miami" in a dark translucent pill.
- 2.0–3.0s: cards slide out; "WITHOUT LEAVING" → "DUBAI?" yellow, bigger.
- Changes in 3s: 6.

## Structure
- Presenter segments with 3 different backgrounds (gym → glass wall → kitchen/showroom) = visual chapters.
- Overlays: glass HUD panel with 5 stars + rising yellow line chart ("ONE OF THE MOST SUCCESSFUL DEVELOPERS"); thumbnails popping onto open palms ("HERE IN DUBAI") with white glow flash; yellow tag words ("WESTERN LIFESTYLE", "ELEGANCE", "& ENERGY", "PAYMENT PLAN", "WITHOUT ANY STRESS", "CONTACT ME NOW") always two-tier: small white caps over big yellow italic.
- Full-screen property renders with thin serif titles ("FLOW", "world class amenities", "designed to make every day…") and slow drone-like push.
- Info cards on a **soft yellow flower-gradient bg**: photo thumbnails pop one by one; calendar-icon pill "A flexible three years / 40% Post handover"; 3D house icon + "AFTER HANDOVER".
- Ending: presenter in sales office, "CONTACT ME NOW" + "AND STEP INTO YOUR MIAMI LIFESTYLE".

## Engine needs
Push-pin card fly-in with tilt + 2.5D parallax, person matte for cards behind head, palm-anchored thumbnails (hand tracking), glass HUD chart, two-tier yellow tag system, info-pill cards on gradient.

## For Benzadid
Strong for **service/offer reels**: pinned cards of client websites flying behind Shadly; payment/price pills ("$550 Starter", "$800 Pro"). Needs clean background or matte.
