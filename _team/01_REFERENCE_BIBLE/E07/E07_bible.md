# E07 — Luxury real-estate ad, "Hey Siri" concept (720×1280, 30fps, 50.8s)
Format: **Location/product ad: presenter walks through spaces, AI-assistant narrative.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01–02.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0s: model walking toward camera on lawn, towers behind; persistent UI: two logos top corners, location pin "KHARGHAR" top-centre, "ACTUAL SHOT" tag left.
- 0.2s: **Siri orb** (animated iridescent sphere) appears at her knees and stays all video = narrative device; a rainbow audio-wave strip pulses across the frame at hip level when "Siri" listens.
- 0.6s: "Hey" small + "Siri" huge **water/liquid-texture** blue letters, placed BEHIND her body (person matte).
- 1.4–2.8s: "show me a" types char-by-char small, then "Life" giant in the same liquid texture, "beyond ordinary" small under it.
- Changes in 3s: 5. Model mid-stride the whole time.

## Structure
- Dialogue concept: she asks, "Siri" answers with a location → hard cut to drone/interior shot with one keyword ("sunlight", "skyline", "swim", "workout", "Club Athena", "podium garden", "spa", "screenings", "celebrations", "every mood").
- Drone top-downs & wide lens interiors every 1–2s; the model reappears in each space (continuity of person).
- Transitions: whip/blur with letters smearing ("Sure" stretches into the next shot), match-cuts on movement.
- Ending: dark hero wide of the complex, title "SAI WORLD EMPIRE / KHARGHAR" then "WHERE EVERY DAY / FEELS EXTRAORDINARY" with letter-spaced serif-less caps.

## Typography
- One keyword per shot, lower-middle or behind subject, rounded geometric sans, **texture/gradient fill tied to the shot** (water texture for pool, glass for gym), small connector above.

## Engine needs
Persistent brand HUD (logos, pin, tag), animated orb component (Lottie/shader), audio-reactive wave strip, textured text fill (video/texture inside letters via background-clip), smear transition, person matte.

## For Benzadid
Production-heavy (drone, model). Usable idea: **"Hey AI, show me a doctor's website that…" orb narrative** over screen recordings of client sites — cheap and on-brand.
