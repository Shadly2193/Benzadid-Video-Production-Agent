- Location A (outdoor, daylight, holding a yellow book "HOOKED" = prop as hook).
- 0.0s: "Here's a" white thin sans **drops in from above top edge** (slide down + fade).
- 0.5s: "SECRET" big condensed white with **horizontal speed-lines/strike bars** sliding left-right, "STRATEGY" small under it = "secret" glitch-stamp. Sits on chest, behind nothing.
