# E04 — Paper-collage money explainer, faceless (720×1280, 60fps, 16.8s)
Format: **Faceless editorial collage, cream grid paper.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0s: B&W crowd photo in rounded square **slides in from right with drop shadow** (no empty frame at all).
- 0.5s: "MOST" condensed grey sans drops in from top (mask reveal), 0.9s "PEOPLE" big + "THINK" tiny under it → stacked lockup beside the image.
- 1.3s: hard swap to new composition: money bag **swings in on a rope from top**, cash stack slides from right, reaching B&W hands rise from bottom edge, crumpled bills drift in corners → 4 objects enter from 4 edges at once.
- 2.6s: hard swap: B&W man in suit falling diagonally, cash bundle below.
- Changes in 3s: 6. Each "page" holds 0.8–1.3s.

## Visual system
- Constant cream (#FAF3DC) background with faint grid → world never changes, only collage pieces (same rule as E14).
- Halftone/B&W cut-outs of people + **full-colour money** = colour only on the subject of the sentence.
- Type: tall condensed grotesk (Bebas/Anton) in dark grey, words placed BEHIND/through objects (cash stack covers half of "MONEY IS THING"); tiny serif connectors ("chase it", "the more you"); yellow highlight bars sliding in; script hand font at end ("they own it.").
- Dark spotlight scene once (man reading newspaper under stage light, "FEW EVER SPEEK" stacked italic, "but here's the truth" with yellow tag) = contrast break.
- Framed portraits (Jobs, Zuckerberg, Gates, Jack Ma) with soft drop shadow, cross-dissolving one into the next inside the same frame.
- Objects keep **gentle continuous motion** (swing, drift, fall) — nothing is static.

## Engine needs
Cut-out asset library (money, hands, falling man, bags), rope-swing physics (spring easing), text-behind-object layering, slide-in-from-edge with shadow, crossfade portraits in frame, yellow bar wipe.

## For Benzadid
Ideal for "money/time" topics (৳ notes instead of $) — e.g. "clinics lose ৳… every month without a site".
