Format: **Seated talking-head, desk, numbered "7 steps" list with heavy UI overlays.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01–03.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0s: hands clasped, no text (0.1s only).
- 0.1–0.6s: "IT / TOOK / ME" heavy italic caps (Gotham-Black-italic-like, white) build word by word **while a thin neon-yellow line swoops in and a pen-nib icon writes** next to it.
- 0.6–1.2s: second line "FOUR YEARS" in **neon yellow #E6FF4D** = number/claim colour.
- 1.3s: line replaced "TO LEARN / THIS" (THIS ~2× size).
- 2.0s: **glowing ring flash** on chest → ring becomes a **countdown timer "60s → 59s → 58s"** that keeps ticking = retention device (viewer waits for the end).
- Changes in 3s: 6.

## Caption system
- Chest position, 1–2 lines, heavy italic caps; small line + big line; key words colour-coded: yellow = numbers/steps, **red = negative** ("THAT HARD", "DON'T EVER", "NOT THE TOOLS", "ELSE MATTERS").
- Step numbers in yellow circular badges that sit left of the step title ("1 SELECTING A PROGRAM").

- App icons (Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut) in rounded tiles pop in a row, label "IT'S FREE" with hand-drawn arrow, sticker "BEGINNER FRIENDLY".
