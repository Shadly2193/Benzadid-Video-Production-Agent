# E05 — Talking head ↔ full-screen kinetic typography cards (720×1280, 25fps, 40.2s)
Format: **Talking-head (dark, moody) alternating with full-screen typography cards.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0–0.7s: face, word-by-word subtitle "Your typography / isn't bad, your" (small white sans, chest, 2 lines; next word pre-shown dim grey then turns white = karaoke reveal).
- 0.7–1.0s: **colour-gradient flash transition**: face washed red→orange, full white flash, yellow/orange diagonal gradient, back to red-tinted face (4 frames each) = "light-leak burst".
- 1.9s: hard cut to cream card: giant vertical orange "REFERENCES" **scrolls up through frame** over a faint outlined repeat of the same word (echo pattern background).
- 2.6s: back to face, smiling "Let's understand this better".
- Changes in 3s: 5.

## Structure / rhythm
- Face segments 2–5s; typography cards 1.5–3s each; ~50/50 split.
- Cards: cream #E9E4DE background, brand pair **blue #2F4FD8 + orange #F06A2E**, rounded heavy grotesk (Gilroy/Clash-like) + light sans for connectors.
- Card animations: lockups build top→down ("CLEAN / Hierarchy / Big / Medium / Small" with a vertical guide line drawing down), staggered word slide-ins, oval stroke drawn around "FONT pairing", tiny tilted tag "Brand personality", bullet list builds (· Hierarchy · Spacing · Pairing), example posters fan in with shadows, number "30 seconds" in giant orange over face.
- CTA card: "comment TYPOGRAPHY" + illustrated character peeking in.

## Captions on face

## Look
