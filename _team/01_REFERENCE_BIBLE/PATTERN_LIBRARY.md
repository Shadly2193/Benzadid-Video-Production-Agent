## B. Hook patterns (first 3 seconds) — target ≥ 4 visual changes in 3s, never an empty first frame
H1 Start mid-gesture/mid-walk, no greeting (E02, E16, E07).
H2 Claim lockup builds word-by-word: small connector + giant keyword (E02 "que com / APENAS R$20", E16 "LAST YEAR").
H3 Number/money first with counter or object (E13 $29→$1,000, E11 0.5 MILLION, E06 countdown 60s).
H4 Focus pull blur→sharp on subject + whip-tilt to next page (E01).
H5 Sentence assembled out of order in a dark HUD with camera pull-back (E03).
H6 Object replaces a letter in the keyword (E08 "br🧠ins").
H7 Person silhouette flash (red fill → white → real) + shock marks (E15).
H8 Persistent device that pays off later: countdown ring (E06), Siri orb (E07).
H9 Prop in hand / photo lands on palm (E16, E12).

## C. Caption systems
C1 Two-tier: running small subtitle on chest + headline keyword at top (E02).
C2 Clusters above/below a centred object, 2–4 size tiers, typed (E14, E10).
C3 Heavy italic caps, colour-coded meaning: yellow = numbers, red = negative (E06, E12).
C4 Karaoke: next word pre-shown dim, lights up when spoken (E05).
C5 Minimal small white sans, one phrase (premium/cinematic) (E11).
C6 Keyword behind the head (matte) (E16, E07, E02).
C7 Text on path / rotated along body (E15).
Universal: ≤ 2 lines per tier, inside 4:5 safe zone, clear all text on shot change, accent colour on 1 word max per line.

## D. Camera / transitions
T1 Punch-in 100↔115–125% on sentence boundaries (jump-cut disguise) (E02, E06).
T2 Whip tilt/pan with motion blur between "pages" (E01, E07).
T3 Light-leak / gradient flash (E05), white flash (E02, E03).
T4 Colour-state emphasis: B&W, red mono, colour wash (E06, E15).
T5 Pixel dissolve on object only (E14).
T6 Iris/spotlight, keyhole doors, ink blot wipes (E10).
T7 Continuous camera move through a constant world (E14, E04; our WebTrack/portfolio glide).
T8 Defocus flash 2–3 frames at topic shift (E02).

## F. Colour rules
- One accent colour per video, used on ≤ 1 word per line (red E01/E13/E15, yellow E06/E12, orange+blue E05, lilac E16).
- Faceless styles: constant background (cream E04, white E08, periwinkle E14, grey E10, black E13).
- Talking heads: natural skin, contrast from wardrobe/wall (E02 coral wall, E05 dark + orange jacket, E11 warm tungsten).
