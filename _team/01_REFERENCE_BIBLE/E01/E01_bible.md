# E01 — Editorial B&W motion explainer, faceless (720×1280, 30fps, 28.0s)
Format: **Faceless motion graphic / editorial collage.** Voice-over only. Evidence: hook_0-3s_10fps.jpg, sheet_2fps_01.jpg.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0–0.4s: subject cut-out photo (B&W) **racks from heavy blur to sharp** while the whole frame is tilted ~15° on a paper spotlight vignette = "camera finds the subject".
- 0.3–0.8s: script font name "Alex Hormozi's" types/fades in above head, ink-black, slight RGB split.
- 0.8–1.4s: red bold sans "$100 million" builds left→right, then tiny "hook" in red after it (big/small pairing).
- 2.4–3.0s: **camera whip-tilts up with motion blur** and the next scene (grid paper) slides in from below = continuous-camera transition, no hard cut.
- Changes in 3s: 4 (focus pull, title, money line, whip transition).

## Visual system
- Palette: B&W only + **one accent red** (#E0102A-ish); final CTA in green. Colour = emphasis, never decoration.
- Background: paper texture, grid-paper, soft radial vignette, dark corners; constant "desk/paper" world.
- Fonts: script (Great-Vibes-like) for the narrative small words, heavy grotesk for punch words, red highlight bar behind a word ("his hook").
- Global FX: subtle **RGB chromatic split** on edges, film grain, defocused foreground shapes (dark triangles/petals) drifting past = depth/parallax.
- Objects: realistic cut-outs (eye, ear, brain, fishhook, money fan, puzzle piece, diamond, sticky notes on red-string board, chat-bubble icons) — **one literal object per idea** ("hits your eye" → eyeball; "your ear" → ear; "brain" → brain; "hook" → fishhook dropping in).
- Arrows hand-drawn pointing at the object.

## Rhythm
- New idea/object every ~1.5–2s; transitions are camera moves (whip-pan, tilt, blur) between "pages" rather than cuts.
- Text reveals word-by-word synced to voice; full sentence stays ≤ 2 lines.
- Black "breath" scene at ~15s ("It's not decoration / It is a pacing tool") = contrast reset mid-video.
- Ending CTA: "DM 'grow' for the blueprint" with chat-bubble icons sliding in.

## Engine needs
Focus-pull (blur→sharp), whip-tilt transition with motion blur, RGB split, paper/grid backgrounds, red highlight bar, script+grotesk font pair, object cut-out library (anatomy, money, hooks, icons), hand-drawn arrows, foreground blur shapes.
