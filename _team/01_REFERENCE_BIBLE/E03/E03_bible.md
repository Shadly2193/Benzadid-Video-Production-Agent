# E03 — Cinematic tech hook → talking head (720×1280, 30fps, 37.9s)
Format: **Motion-graphic cold open (~6s) then talking-head with inserts.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01.

## Hook 0–3s (pure motion graphics, no face)
- 0.0–0.3s: black. 0.3–0.6s: "if you're still" builds word-by-word, **each word blur→sharp + slight upward drift**, soft glow (bloom) on white text.
- 0.9s: "doing" lands below; 1.3s big "outreach" lands **above** (sentence assembles out of reading order → curiosity).
- 1.3–2.0s: UI chips (blue filter pill, chat bubble "Hey, I'm a video editor…") **slide in from the edges, out of focus → in focus** = depth-of-field UI.
- 2.0–3.0s: **camera dollies back & rotates**: the whole text cluster shrinks into a dark HUD grid with thin lines and a growing orange curve line; new big word "editor." lands bottom = the frame keeps zooming out to reveal a bigger "map".
- Changes in 3s: ~7. Fastest hook in the set.

## Structure
- 0–6s cold open in a dark 3D HUD world (grid, ticks, rulers, glowing dots, orange bezier line drawn on), blue flash-frame "behind" at 4s.
- 6s first face shot "tell you why" → talking head with word-by-word captions on chest.
- Interleaved full-screen inserts every 3–5s: lens-flare + cursor scene ("thousands of editors"), cream paper world with chat bubbles inside a black circle, red/orange scribble lines, "ONE MORE" blue italic condensed word, neon light-streak overlay over face ("what you"), purple colour-wash on face for emphasis.
- Ending: picture shrinks into small card with giant quoted "2025" CTA ("comment '2025'").

## Captions
- Talking-head: white bold sans (SF/Inter-like), 2 lines max, **left-of-centre on chest**, tight tracking, word-by-word.
- Graphic scenes: mixed sizes, small connectors + big keyword, soft glow.

## FX vocabulary
Blur-in words, bloom glow, DOF UI chips, camera pull-back reveal, drawn bezier lines, lens flare sweep, light-streak overlay, colour-wash flash (blue/purple), white flash transition, paper/cream "world" switch, card-shrink ending.

## Engine needs
3D camera on text cluster (Three.js/CSS3D), per-word blur-in + bloom, drawn SVG paths, flare/light-leak overlays (free packs), colour-wash flash.
