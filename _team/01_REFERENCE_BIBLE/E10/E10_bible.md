# E10 — Podcast clip + editorial motion inserts (720×1280, 30fps, 50.9s)
Format: **Podcast/interview clip with long full-screen motion-graphic explainer in the middle.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01–02.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0–0.3s: speaker mid-sentence, **slow push-in + slight pan** on a 3/4 angle podcast shot (cool grey grade, mic visible).
- 0.3s: "Because" small italic serif appears on chest; 1.0–1.6s "our brains" big bold condensed sans, **letters rise one by one with blur** (per-character stagger).
- 1.8–3.0s: "can't take in all the" small italic → "available" medium bold → "information." large bold → a 4-tier stacked lockup (size hierarchy = meaning hierarchy).
- No graphics yet — hook is purely the **claim + caption hierarchy**; first insert at ~3.5s.

## Structure
- 0–3.5s podcast → 3.5–40s **continuous faceless explainer** (voice continues) → back to podcast for the conclusion.
- Explainer world: light grey bg, **radial vignette + soft grey sweeping arcs**, one realistic object per idea:
  brain ("11000000 bits" counter in black tag box counting up) → book "War and Peace" → eye in rounded card → rounded video-card B-roll (person playing guitar, woman's face) → brain inside concentric rings with text on a circular path → **black frame with spotlight circle** ("But conscious awareness / is only 50 bits") → clock (sentence per…) → **particle/dust swirl** text assembly ("Your brain, your conscious mind just can't handle") → keyhole wipe (black doors open) "filter information to this tiny keyhole of attention" → ink-blot transition → gears "we call this predictive".
- Transitions: element-to-element morphs (brain shrinks into spotlight), wipes (doors, ink spread), whip blur — rarely a plain cut.

## Captions
- Serif italic small (connectors) + bold sans large (keywords), mixed in stacked lockups; centre or chest; black on grey in explainer, white on podcast.
- Numbers in **black-tag boxes** with counting animation.

## Engine needs
Per-character blur rise, number counter tag, text-on-circle path, spotlight iris transition, particle text assembly, keyhole/door wipe, ink-blot wipe (video matte overlay), rounded B-roll cards.
