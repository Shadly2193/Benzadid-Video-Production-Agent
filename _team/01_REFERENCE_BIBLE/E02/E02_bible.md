# E02 — Static talking-head, serif captions, coloured wall (720×1280, 30fps, 72.5s)
Format: **Talking-head, seated/standing, ONE locked-off shot.** Closest to Shadly's own setup. Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01/02.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0s: no text, mid-gesture (hands already moving toward camera) — starts mid-energy, no "hi".
- 0.2s: first caption word "Você" appears on chest, white serif (Libre-Baskerville-like, ~5% width), word-by-word build.
- 1.0s: small serif connector "que com" top-left ABOVE the head, then **"APENAS" big condensed serif (~11% height) at top**, then "R$20" added → the money/claim line lives at the top, the running sentence stays on the chest.
- 2.4s: dotted underline animates under "R$20" (emphasis mark).
- Only 1 camera cut in 72s per scene detector → the "edit" is entirely captions + punch-ins + inserts.

## Caption system (the core of this style)
- Two tiers: (1) running subtitle on chest, white serif, 1–3 words, replaces itself; (2) **headline keyword at top of frame**, big all-caps serif, with tiny connector words above it ("que com / APENAS R$20", "em algo / PROFISSIONAL.", "uma cena / AMADORA").
- Accent colours per meaning: yellow "A ILUMINAÇÃO", red "AMADORA", white "PROFISSIONAL" with a hand-drawn halo/oval swoosh around it.
- Some keywords sit **behind the head** (top tier overlaps hairline).

## Punch-ins / camera
- Digital zoom between ~100% and ~125% every sentence (visible scale changes in sheet: wide ↔ tight framing) — jump-cut disguise.
- **Defocus flash**: frame goes blurry for 2–3 frames at topic shifts (rows 5–6 "sabe o que acontece?", "O jeito que").
- One **white flash + blur whip** transition (row 3) into the "before/after" section.

## Inserts
- Picture-in-picture "before" screenshots: small card top-left with handwritten labels "Escuro / Amador / Sem vida" listed one by one, "?" doodle; then a second card top-right ("foi rápido") = proof B-roll without leaving the talking head.

## Colour / set
- Saturated coral-red wall, practical tube light behind (orange), black shirt → subject pops; warm, contrasty grade.

## Engine needs
Two-tier caption layout, serif type pairing, per-keyword accent colour, hand-drawn halo/underline SVG strokes, PiP card with handwritten list, auto punch-in on sentence boundaries, defocus flash transition.
