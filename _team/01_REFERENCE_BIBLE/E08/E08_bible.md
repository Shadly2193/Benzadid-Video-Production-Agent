# E08 — Minimal white motion graphic, brain bullets (720×1280, 60fps, 9.9s)
Format: **Faceless minimal motion graphic, single object, white studio.** Evidence: hook sheet, sheet_2fps_01.

## Hook 0–3s
- 0.0–0.5s: word "brains" fades grey→black, **a real B&W brain photo replaces the letter "a"** (object-in-word); tiny "our" above-left.
- 0.2–1.0s: thin grey vertical pole/line grows down from the brain like a lollipop stick; small zig-zag squiggle doodles pop in corners and wiggle.
- 1.0–2.0s: sub-line builds word by word in tiny bold sans: "operates in two distinct" — words appear at staggered baselines, then **"modes" in red** (#D0021B) larger = single accent word.
- 2.2s: brain **turns colour (pink/red 3D)** and falls/rolls down out of the word, headline fades; 2.6s camera pans to new layout: big red brain, curved grey arc behind, dot grid.
- Changes in 3s: 5. Very calm, 60fps smoothness.

## Rest (3–10s)
- Brain crops off frame edges (top-right / left) as camera re-frames — object is used as a **compositional anchor**, moving to make room for text.
- Bullet lists in elegant **italic serif** (Playfair-italic-like) build one per beat: "· Fast · Automatic · Unconscious" / "· Slow · Conscious · Effortful", previous bullet greyed as next lands (focus emphasis).

## Visual rules
- White/very light grey gradient bg, soft grey arc shapes, faint dot grid; black type; ONE accent (red); one hero object, photo-real.
- Handle "@frameco" small top-right.
- Motion: slow eases, nothing snaps; 60fps.

## Engine needs
Object-replaces-letter lockup, growing line stroke, wiggling doodle SVGs, 3D-ish object fall/roll (2D transforms + shadow), italic serif bullet builder with dim-previous, re-framing camera.
